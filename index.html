DRAFT PHASE

Each round begins with the Draft. You will make 24 picks total — 3 ships, 3 pilots, and 18 equipment parts — building the squadron you will fly into combat.

SHIPS — PICKS 1–3

Each offer shows 3 ship cards. Pick one. Ships you don't pick are gone for this round. Your three ships define your squad's size, faction, slot layout, and inherent traits like Solar Wings or Combat Transformation.

PILOTS — PICKS 4–6

Each offer shows 3 pilot cards. Pilots bring Piloting, Gunnery, and Systems stats that randomize each combat round, plus Certifications that determine Familiarity with their assigned ship. A certified pilot flies better — a pilot flying their specific sub-model flies best of all.

EQUIPMENT — PICKS 7–24

18 picks of weapons, engines, shields/armor, and systems. The first 9 offers are structured to guarantee a balanced spread of part types. The final 9 are open.

Each part has a colored pip diagram. To attach a part directly to a ship, the part's highest pip count edge must match both the color and link count of the ship slot. Parts can also chain onto other parts — as long as each connection matches the color and pip count of the outward-facing edge. Mismatched non-highest edges are legal but cost Excess Power each round.

THE SHOP

During the Build Phase you can trade any drafted part 1-for-1 for a basic Shop item in Small, Medium, or Large. The Shop is a safety net, not a strategy.
