# WING LEADER
## Player Rules — v0.8

---

# OVERVIEW

Wing Leader is a Star Wars-themed autobattler. You manage a squadron of three starships, each assigned a pilot and outfitted with parts. Your decisions happen in the Draft and Build phases. Once you lock your loadout, combat resolves automatically and you watch the outcome play out.

A full game consists of 3 Rounds: **Draft > Build > Combat**

Each Round you are matched against one other player on the same Round as you, drawn from the global player pool. Points accumulate across all three Loops. Most points after Round 3 wins.

---

# GAME STRUCTURE

## Rounds

Each Round contains one match against a same-Round opponent. You draft and build independently.

## Scoring

| Result | Points |
|---|---|
| Win | 3 |
| Draw | 1 |
| Loss | 0 |

Maximum: 9 points. Tiebreaker: total damage dealt. If still tied: shared victory.

## Between Loops

- Ships, pilots, and parts carry forward.
- All damage resets.
- Rounds 2 and 3 use an Upgrade Draft.

# SHIPS

## Sizes

- **Small:** +2 base Excess Power. High maneuverability, low durability.
- **Medium:** +1 base Excess Power.
- **Large:** +0 base Excess Power.

## Connectors

| Category | Color | Ship Edge |
|---|---|---|
| Weapons | Green | Top |
| Systems | Purple | Right |
| Engines | Blue | Bottom |
| Shields / Armor | Yellow | Left |

| Size | Connectors Per Edge |
|---|---|
| Large | 3 |
| Medium | 2 |
| Small | 1 |

TIE-class ships and the Jedi Starfighter replace their Left Yellow (Shields) edge with an additional Purple (Systems) edge.

## Part Connector Variations

Each part comes in five connector variations. The facing side connects toward the ship or parent part.

| Variation | Facing | Opposite | Other Two | Fits Ship Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | 1 | 1 | 1 each | Small |
| V2 | 2 | 2 | 1 each | Medium |
| V3 | 2 | 1 | 1 each | Medium |
| V4 | 3 | 2 | 1 each | Large |
| V5 | 3 | 1 | 1 each | Large |

## Connection Rules

**Color must match on the highest side.** The side of a part with the most connectors must always match the color of whatever it touches — whether that is a ship edge or another part. Mismatched highest sides block placement.

**Other sides can mismatch at a cost.** Non-highest-side connections between different-colored edges are legal but cost +1 Excess Power per round for the ship. Costs stack.

## Mitigation Cap by Weapon Size

Shields and Armor cannot absorb as much damage from larger weapons.

| Weapon Variation | Max Mitigation Per Hit |
|---|---|
| V1 | No cap |
| V2 or V3 | 2 |
| V4 or V5 | 1 |


## Ship Traits

Every ship may have Advantages and Drawbacks that reflect its canonical design and role. These are fixed properties of the ship — they cannot be removed, modified, or drafted around. They apply in addition to all other rules.

### Advantages

**Solar Wings (X)** — This ship's solar collector wings generate +X Excess Power each round, in addition to all other sources. Cannot be combined with Removed Fairings.

**Combat Transformation** — This ship reconfigures at the start of combat (S-Foils locking to attack position, fan blades deploying, etc.). It automatically activates first in Round 1 Initiative. If multiple ships have Combat Transformation, ties between them are broken by a normal Initiative roll.

**Removed Fairings (X)** — Exposed engine components generate +X Excess Power each round. This ship cannot equip Armor of any kind. Cannot be combined with Solar Wings.

**Rotating Cockpit (X)** — The pilot's seat or cockpit rotates to maintain orientation during maneuvers. +X to Comfort each round.

**Bomber (X)** — This ship's frame is built to carry and deploy heavy ordnance. Ordinance weapons installed on this ship may fire X additional times per match (instead of being single-use).

**Gunship** — This ship has a dedicated secondary weapons position (rear gunner, ventral turret, etc.). It may equip one Weapon beyond what its Engine capacity would normally allow. That extra weapon draws no power from the Engine.

**Tough (X)** — X of this ship's installed items have one extra hit point. The first hit dealt to each protected item is ignored entirely. They can still be damaged and destroyed by subsequent hits.

### Drawbacks

**Blind Spots** — This ship's hull, wing configuration, or cockpit placement creates significant rearward visibility gaps. Attacks against this ship from Tailed position gain one attack quality step improvement. A future pilot trait (Ignore Blind Spots) will be available for Force-sensitive and sensor-enhanced pilots.

**Fragile (X)** — This ship's construction is lighter or more exposed than standard. At the start of each match, X randomly selected installed items begin at DS1.

## Assembly Rules

**Highest connector side first.** The side with the most connectors connects first and must color-match its attachment point.

**Power constraints.** Engines define how many powered items they support. Weapons, Shields, Armor, and Systems consume power.

**Mismatch cost.** Each non-highest-side connection between different-colored edges costs -1 Excess Power per round.

| State | Effect |
|---|---|
| DS0 | Full function. |
| DS1 | Item's primary value reduced by 1. |
| DS2 | Severely impaired. -1 to Excess Power. |
| DS3 | Destroyed. Removed from ship. |

# DRAFT PHASE

## Round 1: Full Draft

Draft from your own private decks simultaneously with all other players.

### Ships (repeat 3 times)
Draw 3 ships. Select 1. Discard the other 2 to your Round Discard pile.

### Pilots (repeat 3 times)
Draw 3 pilots. Select 1. Return unchosen pilots to their rarity pools.

### Parts (repeat 18 times)
Draw 3 parts. Select 1. Discard the other 2 to your Round Discard pile.

## Rounds 2 and 3: Upgrade Draft

Your squadron carries in. Replace as much or as little as you want.

### Step 1: Seeded Rewards
If your roster meets any seeded reward prerequisites, you receive an additional pick-one-of-three for that reward before the main draft. Declining forfeits it permanently.

### Step 2: Upgrade Ships (up to 3, optional)
Draw 3 ships. Select 1 to replace a current ship slot. Discard the rest and the replaced ship.

### Step 3: Upgrade Pilots (up to 3, optional)
Same process as Round 1. Replaced pilots return to their rarity pools.

### Step 4: Upgrade Parts (up to 18, optional)
Draw 3 parts. Select 1. Discard the rest and the replaced part. Always end with exactly 18 parts.

## Round Discard Rule

All discarded and replaced components shuffle back into your personal decks at the start of each new Round.

# BUILD PHASE

All players build simultaneously and independently. Take as much time as you need.

## Step 1: Configure Loadouts

Distribute all 18 parts across your three ships freely. No minimum or maximum per ship. Follow all Assembly Rules.

**Each ship must have at least one Engine and at least one Weapon before you can proceed to combat.**

## Step 2: Shop (Optional)

Trade any drafted part 1-for-1 for a Gray Shop item. Shop items come in three sizes: Small (V1), Medium (V3), and Large (V5) — choose the variant that fits your ship's connector count. Always end with exactly 18 parts.

## Step 3: Set Behavioral Profiles and Target Priorities

For each ship, choose one Behavioral Profile and one Target Priority.

### Behavioral Profiles

**Aggressor** — Maximum aggression. Always uses the highest Excess Power maneuver available.

**Opportunist** — Adapts to the situation. Maintains position when Tailing, escapes when Tailed, seeks the best target when OOP.

**Escort** — Names one specific friendly ship as its ward. Set during this step. Intercepts enemies Tailing the ward. Escapes first if currently Tailed. Reverts to Opportunist if ward is destroyed.

**Predator** — Pursues a designated enemy ship relentlessly. The specific target is set at combat setup when you know your opponent's ships, not during this step. Escapes first if currently Tailed. Reverts to Opportunist if target is destroyed.

**Survivor** — Self-preservation first. Escapes when Tailed. Only attacks when Tailing with confidence. Avoids OOP engagements. Reverts to Opportunist if last friendly ship active.

### Target Priorities

**Highest Threat** — Target the enemy Tailing a friendly or dealing the most cumulative damage.

**Lowest Health** — Target the most damaged enemy.

**Best Position** — Target whichever enemy this ship has the best positional relationship with.

**Designated Target** — Name one specific enemy ship at combat setup. Attack it until destroyed, then revert to Best Position.

## Step 4: Assign Pilots

Assign each of your three pilots to one of your three ships. Any pilot can fly any ship.

_Pilots are called to their ships after the mission parameters are locked. Familiarity scores are shown live during assignment to help you make the best pairing decision._

## Step 5: Excess Power Preview

| Source | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Small ship | +2 |
| Medium ship | +1 |
| Large ship | +0 |
| Each unused powered engine slot | +1 |
| Certified pilot on this ship | +1 |
| Each DS2 item | -1 |
| Each color-mismatched non-highest-side connection | -1 |
| Active system Excess Power costs | -X |

## Step 6: Lock In

Confirm everything. Combat begins.

# COMBAT PHASE

Fully automated. Watch the round-by-round log. All ships begin Out of Position.

## Positional States
- **Tailing:** You have an enemy in your sights.
- **Tailed:** An enemy has you in their sights.
- **OOP:** No direct engagement.

Chains are legal (A tails B, C tails A). Circular loops are not. A Tailed ship can only use escape maneuvers.

## Maneuvers

### TAILED

| Excess Power | Maneuver | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Snap Roll | Low chance to escape to OOP |
| 1 | S-Thread | Standard chance to escape to OOP |
| 2 | Slip Jibe | High chance to escape to OOP |
| 3 | Pugachev's Cobra | Standard chance to reverse and become Tailing. Low Quality Attack |
| 4 | Tallon Roll | High chance to reverse and become Tailing. Standard Quality Attack |
| 5 | Koiogran Turn | Automatic reversal. Standard Quality Attack |

**Escape Diminishing Returns:** Each consecutive round a ship successfully escapes to OOP, the threshold for its next escape attempt is reduced by 15. Resets when the ship fails an escape or begins a round not being Tailed.

### TAILING

| Excess Power | Maneuver | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Stay on Target | Reduce target's escape chance 1 step. Standard Quality Attack |
| 1 | Displacement Roll | Reduce target's escape chance 2 steps. Low Quality Attack |
| 2 | Vibroknife | Reduce target's escape chance 1 step. High Quality Attack |
| 3 | Exploit Weakness | Reduce target's escape chance 2 steps. High Quality Attack |
| 4 | High Yo | Maintain Tailing. High Quality Attack. Shoot Twice |
| 5 | Dead to Rights | Maintain Tailing. High Quality Attack. Shoot Twice. Target escape reduced 1 Excess Power tier |

### OUT OF POSITION

| Excess Power | Maneuver | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Swooping Attack | Low chance to gain Tailing. Low Quality Attack |
| 1 | Gain the Advantage | Standard chance to gain Tailing. Low Quality Attack |
| 2 | Barrel Roll Attack | High chance to gain Tailing. Standard Quality Attack |
| 3 | Hit and Fade | Standard Quality Attack. No positional change |
| 4 | Ambush | High Quality Attack. No positional change |
| 5 | Blinding Star | High Quality Attack. Cannot be Tailed this round |

## Attack Quality

Roll 1d100 against your Attack Aspect (modified by Comfort).

| Roll | Quality | Damage |
|---|---|---|
| Exceeds Attack Aspect by more than 20 | Low | 1d3 (minimum 2) |
| Within 20 above or any amount below | Standard | 1d6 (minimum 3) |
| 30 or more below Attack Aspect | High | 1d10 (minimum 4) |

OOP snap shots suffer a one-step quality penalty after the roll.

## Mitigation

Shields apply first, then Armor. The Mitigation Cap limits how much any single hit can be reduced based on the weapon that fired it.

| Item | Mitigation | Regenerates |
|---|---|---|
| Weak Shields | 1 | Yes |
| Ablative Armor | 1 | No |
| Reinforced Plating | 1 | No (DS1 resistant) |
| Armor | 2 | No |
| Shields (Uncommon) | 2 | Yes |
| Chromium Armor | 3 | No |
| Shields (Rare) | 3 | Yes |
| Chromium Armor and Shields | 5 | Partial |
| Layered Defense System | 4 | Yes |

## Win Condition

Match ends when all three of one player's ships are Dead in the Water or destroyed.

If Round 12 ends without a winner: most active ships wins. If tied: lowest total Damage State wins. If still tied: Draw.
